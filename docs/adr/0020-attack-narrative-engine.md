# ADR-0020: Attack Narrative Engine (M9 Extension)
**Status:** Accepted. The report's attack narratives section renders from structured attack_chains rows joined with confirmed findings, rather than relying solely on operator-supplied report.json text. Each narrative hop cites its finding ID, title, CVSS v4, CWE, and evidence artifact SHA-256; the chain records entry point, escalation hops in order, impact summary, and tier0_reached. report.json narratives remain as an override/augmentation source, clearly labeled. Rationale: evidence-backed attack sequences are this platform's differentiator — every step of every chain is verifiable against the ledger, which free-text narrative generation cannot provide.
